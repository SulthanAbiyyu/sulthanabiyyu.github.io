Fanesis (Facial Network Analysis)

Introduction Fanesis is a Facial Network Analysis tool that analyzes the faces and behaviors of individuals in a group and creates a visual representation of their relationships. Fanesis uses computer vision and machine learning techniques to detect the emotions and interactions of each individual. It then creates two types of associations: group-based and emotion-based. Group-based associations show how individuals are connected within the group, such as who knows whom, who is close to whom, and who is influential to whom....
