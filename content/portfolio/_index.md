## Work & Impact

Products built at work, in production, with real users.

### OneAnalytics — internal analytics & dashboarding platform

*PT Lautan Natural Krimerindo · 2025–present · Data Analytics, BI*

Before OneAnalytics, getting a number meant filing a request and waiting. I led
the development of a single platform that centralizes business metrics and the
analytical views on top of them, so stakeholders can go look for themselves.

It is now used daily by **50+ users at manager level and above**, including the
Board of Directors, Finance, Sales, and other directorates. The interesting part
of this project was never the charting — it was figuring out which metrics
different directorates actually argue about, and making those the ones that are
easiest to find.

### Self-service analytics chatbot

*PT Lautan Natural Krimerindo · 2025 · LLM, RAG*

A data-grounded internal chatbot that answers business questions directly
against company data, replacing the manual request-and-wait process.

**Time-to-insight dropped from 25 hours to 3 minutes.** The hard problem here is
grounding: an analytics chatbot that is confidently wrong is worse than no
chatbot, so most of the work went into retrieval and into constraining what the
model is allowed to claim.

### Automatic dustiness powder quality control

*PT Lautan Natural Krimerindo · 2025 · Computer Vision, Segmentation*

An automated QC system that analyzes video of falling powder and measures the
resulting dust plume. The QC team uses it on **100+ powder samples daily**.

What I'm most happy about is the second-order effect: the computer vision
measurement became a **new quantitative QC standard** at the company. Dustiness
used to be judged by eye. Now it has a number.

### Receipt & invoice recognition

*Vobis.io · Jan–Mar 2025 · OCR, LLM*

An automated document recognition system for receipts and invoices, reaching
**92% accuracy** on internal benchmarks. The approach is a hybrid: native OCR
for what OCR is good at, an LLM for the parsing and layout reasoning it isn't.
Optimized to run on edge devices.

I also built an LLM chatbot there that personalizes responses using hybrid
RAG — semantic plus keyword retrieval — grounded in user-specific information.

### Data pipeline reliability — 1st Place QCC

*Lautan Luas Tbk. & PT Lautan Natural Krimerindo · Continuous Improvement, Data Engineering*

A critical pipeline running **~27K executions per week** kept breaching SLA, with
delays of up to 6 hours. I reworked its monitoring, error handling, and the
operational process around it, and got it to **100% SLA compliance**.

Won **1st Place** at the company level, then **1st Place at the Lautan Luas
Group level**.

## Research

### Anak Baik: A Low-Cost Approach to Curate Indonesian Ethical and Unethical Instructions

*SEALP 2025 — Second Workshop in South East Asian Language Processing, ACL · pp. 52–62*
Sulthan Abiyyu Hakim, Rizal Setya Perdana, Tirana Noor Fatyanosa

Indonesian LLMs are bad at telling ethical instructions from unethical ones, and
there was no Indonesian dataset to fix that. So we made one.

*Anak Baik* — "good boy" — is an Indonesian instruction-response dataset for
supervised fine-tuning, containing both ethical and unethical responses so a
model can learn the difference rather than just memorize refusals. Fine-tuning
Komodo and Cendol on it with LoRA produced significant gains in ethical
decision-making, validated with BLEU and ROUGE.

The whole point was *low-cost*: ethical alignment work for Indonesian shouldn't
require a frontier-lab budget.

[Paper](https://aclanthology.org/2025.sealp-1.5/) ·
[Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/SulthanAbiyyu/anak-baik) ·
[Model](https://huggingface.co/SulthanAbiyyu/anak-baik-7b) ·
[Code](https://github.com/SulthanAbiyyu/anak-baik-code)

### Klasifikasi Citra Generasi AI menggunakan Fine Tuning pada Residual Network

*JTIIK — Jurnal Teknologi Informasi dan Ilmu Komputer, Vol. 11 No. 3, June 2024*
Sulthan Abiyyu Hakim et al., supervised by Novanto Yudistira

Detecting whether an image was generated by AI, which matters more every year in
legal and political contexts. We fine-tuned residual networks using a warm-up
learning rate scheduler followed by a linear scheduler, gradient accumulation,
and image augmentation.

ResNet-152 performed best: **F1 score 0.963, loss 0.08**.

[Paper](https://jtiik.ub.ac.id/index.php/jtiik/en/article/view/8118) ·
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.25126/jtiik.1138118)

## Open Source & Side Projects

Things I built because I wanted them to exist.

### Marzuki-7B — an Indonesian LLM, end to end

A full training pipeline on top of Mistral-7B: continued pretraining on
Indonesian Wikipedia, SFT on translated Alpaca and OASST, then DPO alignment.
Three checkpoints published — base, instruct, and aligned.

This is the project where I learned what "end-to-end LLM training" actually
costs you in patience.

[Code](https://github.com/SulthanAbiyyu/Marzuki-7B) ·
[Base](https://huggingface.co/SulthanAbiyyu/marzuki-7B-v2-base) ·
[Instruct](https://huggingface.co/SulthanAbiyyu/marzuki-7B-v2-instruct) ·
[DPO](https://huggingface.co/SulthanAbiyyu/marzuki-7B-v2-dpo)

### Herman — Indonesian JSON-mode dataset

An Indonesian adaptation of NousResearch's Hermes function-calling dataset, for
training LLMs to emit reliable structured JSON from a schema passed in the
system prompt. Built for SFT.

Structured output is the unglamorous thing that decides whether an LLM can be
wired into real software. Indonesian models deserve it too.

[Code](https://github.com/SulthanAbiyyu/herman-json-mode-indo) ·
[Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/SulthanAbiyyu/herman-json-mode)

### Artinya — an LLM translation pipeline

Translation that tries to keep tone, nuance, and cultural meaning intact, via a
three-stage pipeline: describe the source text's style and intent, translate
with that description as context, then evaluate the output on accuracy, clarity,
and preservation of style.

I kept an honest list of open questions in the README — how expensive is this
really, how often do models fail to produce structured output, and does the
whole pipeline even beat a plain translation call. The ablation is the actual
experiment.

[Code](https://github.com/SulthanAbiyyu/artinya)

### Rec2Slide — video recording → PDF slides

Published on PyPI. Samples every *n*-th frame of a lecture or meeting recording,
compares consecutive frames with a similarity score, and reconstructs the deck
as a PDF from the distinct slides.

Small, finished, and shipped — which is rarer than it sounds.

[Code](https://github.com/SulthanAbiyyu/rec2slide) ·
[PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/rec2slide/)
