I’m a Data Scientist and AI Engineer. Most of my day job is turning messy operational problems into something a person can actually act on — a dashboard, a chatbot, a camera pointed at a powder sample. On the side I work on Indonesian language models and small tools that scratch my own itch.

Work & Impact#

Products built at work, in production, with real users.

OneAnalytics — internal analytics & dashboarding platform#

PT Lautan Natural Krimerindo · 2025–present · Data Analytics, BI

Before OneAnalytics, getting a number meant filing a request and waiting. I led the development of a single platform that centralizes business metrics and the analytical views on top of them, so stakeholders can go look for themselves.

It is now used daily by 50+ users at manager level and above, including the Board of Directors, Finance, Sales, and other directorates. The interesting part of this project was never the charting — it was figuring out which metrics different directorates actually argue about, and making those the ones that are easiest to find.

Self-service analytics chatbot#

PT Lautan Natural Krimerindo · 2025 · LLM, RAG

A data-grounded internal chatbot that answers business questions directly against company data, replacing the manual request-and-wait process.

Time-to-insight dropped from 25 hours to 3 minutes. The hard problem here is grounding: an analytics chatbot that is confidently wrong is worse than no chatbot, so most of the work went into retrieval and into constraining what the model is allowed to claim.

Automatic dustiness powder quality control#

PT Lautan Natural Krimerindo · 2025 · Computer Vision, Segmentation

An automated QC system that analyzes video of falling powder and measures the resulting dust plume. The QC team uses it on 100+ powder samples daily.

What I’m most happy about is the second-order effect: the computer vision measurement became a new quantitative QC standard at the company. Dustiness used to be judged by eye. Now it has a number.

Receipt & invoice recognition#

Vobis.io · Jan–Mar 2025 · OCR, LLM

An automated document recognition system for receipts and invoices, reaching 92% accuracy on internal benchmarks. The approach is a hybrid: native OCR for what OCR is good at, an LLM for the parsing and layout reasoning it isn’t. Optimized to run on edge devices.

I also built an LLM chatbot there that personalizes responses using hybrid RAG — semantic plus keyword retrieval — grounded in user-specific information.

Data pipeline reliability — 1st Place QCC#

Lautan Luas Tbk. & PT Lautan Natural Krimerindo · Continuous Improvement, Data Engineering

A critical pipeline running ~27K executions per week kept breaching SLA, with delays of up to 6 hours. I reworked its monitoring, error handling, and the operational process around it, and got it to 100% SLA compliance.

Won 1st Place at the company level, then 1st Place at the Lautan Luas Group level.

Research#

Anak Baik: A Low-Cost Approach to Curate Indonesian Ethical and Unethical Instructions#

SEALP 2025 — Second Workshop in South East Asian Language Processing, ACL · pp. 52–62 Sulthan Abiyyu Hakim, Rizal Setya Perdana, Tirana Noor Fatyanosa

Indonesian LLMs are bad at telling ethical instructions from unethical ones, and there was no Indonesian dataset to fix that. So we made one.

Anak Baik — “good boy” — is an Indonesian instruction-response dataset for supervised fine-tuning, containing both ethical and unethical responses so a model can learn the difference rather than just memorize refusals. Fine-tuning Komodo and Cendol on it with LoRA produced significant gains in ethical decision-making, validated with BLEU and ROUGE.

The whole point was low-cost: ethical alignment work for Indonesian shouldn’t require a frontier-lab budget.

Paper · Dataset · Model · Code

Klasifikasi Citra Generasi AI menggunakan Fine Tuning pada Residual Network#

JTIIK — Jurnal Teknologi Informasi dan Ilmu Komputer, Vol. 11 No. 3, June 2024 Sulthan Abiyyu Hakim et al., supervised by Novanto Yudistira

Detecting whether an image was generated by AI, which matters more every year in legal and political contexts. We fine-tuned residual networks using a warm-up learning rate scheduler followed by a linear scheduler, gradient accumulation, and image augmentation.

ResNet-152 performed best: F1 score 0.963, loss 0.08.

Open Source & Side Projects#

Things I built because I wanted them to exist.

Marzuki-7B — an Indonesian LLM, end to end#

A full training pipeline on top of Mistral-7B: continued pretraining on Indonesian Wikipedia, SFT on translated Alpaca and OASST, then DPO alignment. Three checkpoints published — base, instruct, and aligned.

This is the project where I learned what “end-to-end LLM training” actually costs you in patience.

Code · Base · Instruct · DPO

Herman — Indonesian JSON-mode dataset#

An Indonesian adaptation of NousResearch’s Hermes function-calling dataset, for training LLMs to emit reliable structured JSON from a schema passed in the system prompt. Built for SFT.

Structured output is the unglamorous thing that decides whether an LLM can be wired into real software. Indonesian models deserve it too.

Code · Dataset

Artinya — an LLM translation pipeline#

Translation that tries to keep tone, nuance, and cultural meaning intact, via a three-stage pipeline: describe the source text’s style and intent, translate with that description as context, then evaluate the output on accuracy, clarity, and preservation of style.

I kept an honest list of open questions in the README — how expensive is this really, how often do models fail to produce structured output, and does the whole pipeline even beat a plain translation call. The ablation is the actual experiment.

Code

Rec2Slide — video recording → PDF slides#

Published on PyPI. Samples every n-th frame of a lecture or meeting recording, compares consecutive frames with a similarity score, and reconstructs the deck as a PDF from the distinct slides.

Small, finished, and shipped — which is rarer than it sounds.

Code · PyPI

Batch big data ETL — DuckDB, Spark, Pandas, Postgres#

A dockerized ETL pipeline with DuckDB as the SQL engine, Pandas or Spark as the processing engine, and Postgres as both data lake and warehouse. Dimensional modeling with a Kimball-style star schema.
